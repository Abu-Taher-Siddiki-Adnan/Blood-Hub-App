Child Safety Standards

Effective date: August 2026

At Blood Hub, we are committed to providing a safe environment for all users and maintaining a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page outlines our standards, and how to report a concern.

01

Our Standards

  • Blood Hub strictly prohibits any content, behavior, or activity involving child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Users may not upload, share, request, or distribute any illegal or exploitative content.
  • Any account found engaging in such activities will be permanently suspended or terminated.
  • We cooperate with applicable law enforcement agencies and comply with all relevant laws regarding the reporting of CSAM and child exploitation.
  • Users can report inappropriate content or suspicious behavior through the app or by contacting us directly.
02

Reporting

If you encounter content or behavior that may involve child exploitation or abuse, please report it immediately.

We review reports promptly and take appropriate action.
